Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Fitness AI ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users of our mobile application.

Summary: We prioritize user privacy. We only collect your email address and password strictly for account creation and login. We do not track user history, do not collect location or device data, and do not share any data with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal information strictly necessary for user authentication:

  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier for login and account recovery.
  • Password: Used to secure your account. Passwords are encrypted/hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Unique User ID (UID): An internal identifier generated upon account creation to associate your account with your profile.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To ensure maximum privacy, our application explicitly does NOT collect:

  • Browsing history or app usage logs
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Contacts, photos, or device media
  • Device identifiers for advertising or tracking
  • Financial or payment card details

3. How We Store and Process Data

Your login credentials (email and encrypted password) are transmitted securely using HTTPS/TLS encryption and stored directly in a cloud database powered by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication).

Firebase acts as our infrastructure service provider. Firebase processes this data solely to provide authentication services on our behalf in compliance with industry-standard security practices.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We maintain a strict policy against selling, trading, or sharing user data:

  • No third-party advertising SDKs or tracking pixels are included in our mobile application.
  • No user data is shared with marketing partners or external third parties.
  • Your data is accessed strictly for login authentication within the app.

5. Security of Your Data

We implement appropriate technical and administrative security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data in transit between the application and Firebase is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) / Transport Layer Security (TLS).

6. Account Deletion and Data Retention

You have full control over your personal data:

  • Retention: We retain your email and authentication credentials only for as long as your account remains active.
  • Account Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your account and associated email address at any time by contacting us at masma7oo7@gmail.com. Upon request, your account record in Firebase will be permanently removed.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
